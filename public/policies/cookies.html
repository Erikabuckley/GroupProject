Cookie Statement

Effective as of February 13, 2026

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small file of letters and numbers that is downloaded onto your computer when you visit a website. By saving information about your visit, they make your online experience easier.

How we will use cookies

We may use it to: recognise your computer to visit our website, to improve the website's usability or to analyse the use of our website. We never share or sell your data to third parties, as privacy is our top priority. Some cookies may have a duration of 12 months.

What types of cookies do we use?

A session cookie is used to identify a particular visit to our website. These are used to identify you during a single browsing session and are deleted automatically once you close your browser.


We don't use third-party cookies or sell your data to any third parties.
We use first party cookies for:
  • Functionality: making sure access to the website is optimised
  • Security: to ensure logins are secure and authenticated
  • Cookie consent: remembering your preferences

Your choices

You may choose to enable all cookies or to enable only strictly necessary cookies primarily for the website's functionality.
