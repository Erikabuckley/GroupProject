Cookie Statement

Effective as of February 13, 2026

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small file of letters and numbers that is downloaded onto your computer when you visit a website. By saving information about your visit, they make your online experience easier.

We may use it to recognise your computer to visit our website; to improve the website's usability; or to analyse the use of our website. We never share or sell your data to third parties, as privacy is our top priority.

What types of cookies do we use?

A session cookie is used to identify a particular visit to our website. These are used to identify you during a single browsing session and are deleted automatically once you close your browser. They are necessary cookies as they are required for functionality.


We don't use third-party cookies or sell your data to any third parties.
We use first party cookies for:
  • Functionality: making sure access to the website is optimised and to keep you logged in as you visit the website

Your choices

Session cookies are strictly necessary and cannot be rejected as they aid functionality and performance of the website. Please contact us if you have any queries.
